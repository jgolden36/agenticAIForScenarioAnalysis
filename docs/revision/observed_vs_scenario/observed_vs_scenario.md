# Scenario outputs vs observed crisis-period prices

Observed: World Bank Pink Sheet monthly averages. Pre-crisis baseline = median of 2025-11 to 2026-01; crisis window 2026-02 to 2026-10; the observed peak is the month furthest from the baseline in either direction. Percent changes are each side relative to its own baseline. Monthly averages smooth intra-month spikes, and the scenarios are conditional paths, so this is a plausibility check, not a forecast evaluation.

Runs: may.
